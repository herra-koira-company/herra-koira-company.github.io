ABOUT US

Welcome to Herra Koira Company, the virtual play developer that puts your beloved pets first. We understand the unique bond that exists between you and your furry friend, which is why our focus is on creating digital interspecies play experiences that cater to the actual end user - the animal.

Our development process is guided by feedback from our trusted animal testers, ensuring that we're always moving in the right direction. Our main app, Play With Your Cat, is based on scientific research studying how domestic cats see, react, and interact with virtual play objects.

Our scientific ACI (animal-computer interaction) design approach, combined with a deep respect for our animal friends' needs, has allowed us to create an app that is not only fun and entertaining but also enriches the bond between you and your pet.
